# Mental-Health-Burnout-in-tech

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes employee burnout, identifies key workplace drivers, segments employees into actionable HR personas, and provides business recommendations using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**.

---

## Project Overview

Employee burnout has become one of the biggest challenges affecting workforce productivity, employee retention, and organizational well-being.

This project analyzes employee burnout patterns by exploring workplace conditions, mental health indicators, work-life balance, manager support, and therapy utilization. The analysis transforms raw employee data into actionable HR insights through statistical analysis, SQL business queries, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## Business Objectives

- Identify the major drivers of employee burnout.
- Analyze how work-life balance, sleep, workload, and manager support influence burnout.
- Understand the relationship between mental health and therapy utilization.
- Segment employees into meaningful HR personas.
- Provide data-driven HR recommendations for reducing burnout and improving employee retention.

#  Employee Persona Segmentation

Employees were segmented into mutually exclusive H personas:

-  Flight Risk
-  Early Risk
-  Silent Burnout
-  Resilient Veteran
-  Healthy Performer
- Other

This segmentation enables HR teams to prioritize interventions based on employee risk profiles.

---

#  Key Insights

- Employees sleeping fewer than 5 hours per night reported the highest average burnout.
- Burnout increased as weekly working hours increased.
- Strong manager support was consistently associated with lower burnout.
- Technical job roles reported comparatively higher burnout.
- Employees using therapy generally exhibited higher anxiety and depression scores, suggesting therapy utilization among employees already experiencing mental health challenges.
- Persona segmentation enables targeted HR intervention instead of applying the same strategy to every employee.

---

#  Business Recommendations

- Prioritize intervention for Flight Risk employees.
- Improve manager support through leadership development.
- Monitor employee workload and work-life balance.
- Expand awareness and accessibility of mental health resources.
- Use persona-based HR strategies to improve employee engagement and retention.
